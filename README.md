- [CrisisTwin](https://github.com/Mykeil-tzul/crisistwin-disaster) maps NASA satellite heat detections in California using an automated data pipeline. [Live demo](https://mykeil-tzul.github.io/crisistwin-disaster/)
- [ChargePilot](https://github.com/Mykeil-tzul/chargepilot) compares EV road-trip charging stops by time and cost while keeping a battery reserve. [Live demo](https://mykeil-tzul.github.io/chargepilot/)

- [CourtIQ](https://github.com/Mykeil-tzul/nba-player-predictor) forecasts NBA box-score stats and shows where a Ridge model beats a five-game average and where the average wins. [Live demo](https://nba-player-predictor-bytzul.streamlit.app/)

- [BelizeIQ](https://github.com/Mykeil-tzul/belizeiq) explores Belizean food, destinations, tourism, and district population data, with a research chat that links its sources. [Live demo](https://mykeil-tzul.github.io/belizeiq/)
- [FamilyOS](https://github.com/Mykeil-tzul/family-budget-intelligence) helps you track spending, see what’s due before payday, and explore savings and investing scenarios. [Live demo](https://familos-bymt.streamlit.app/)
- [StatMatch](https://github.com/Mykeil-tzul/statmatch) compares NBA players across careers, seasons, and eras using historical regular-season stats. [Live demo](https://statmatch.streamlit.app/)
- [WonderWorlds](https://github.com/Mykeil-tzul/wonderworlds) gives kids five games for practicing numbers, colors, shapes, and patterns at a pace that adjusts as they play. [Live demo](https://mykeil-tzul.github.io/wonderworlds/)
- [Walk With Jesus Christ](https://github.com/Mykeil-tzul/walkwithjesuschrist) helps readers find Scripture, follow Gospel journeys on a map, and keep a study journal in their browser. [Live demo](https://mykeil-tzul.github.io/walkwithjesuschrist/)
- [KriolBridge AI](https://github.com/Mykeil-tzul/kriolbridge-ai) helps you look up and practice sourced phrases in Belizean Kriol, English, and Spanish and record your own pronunciation. [Live demo](https://mykeil-tzul.github.io/kriolbridge-ai/)
